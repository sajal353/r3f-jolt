# Mannequin

`mannequin.glb` is the Universal Animation Library (Standard) by Quaternius,
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain):
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Trimmed to the Quaternius animations the demos use: `A_TPose`, `Idle_Loop`,
`Walk_Loop`, `Sprint_Loop`, `Jump_Start`, `Jump_Loop`, `Jump_Land`,
`Hit_Chest`, `Hit_Head`, `Death01`, `Swim_Idle_Loop` and `Swim_Fwd_Loop`.

`GetUp_FaceUp` and `GetUp_FaceDown` are not Quaternius's and not CC0: they are
Mixamo's "Standing Up From Face Up" and "Standing Up From Face Down"
(Adobe), applied to this rig on mixamo.com and baked in, trimmed. They are
used under the Mixamo terms of use.
